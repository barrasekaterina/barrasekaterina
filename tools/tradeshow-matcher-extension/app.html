Tradeshow vs CRM Matcher

Everything runs locally in this tab — files are never uploaded anywhere. Pick a tradeshow list plus your CRM exports; the tool auto-detects whether your list has Full Name + Email, + Company Name, + Phone, or all of them, and matches accordingly. No business cards.

How field detection works

  • Full Name is the only required column.
  • Any of Company Name, Phone, Email that are present and non-empty are used as extra matching signals, in any combination.
  • A tradeshow record is only reported as a match when the name matches and at least one other available field also matches.
  • There is no live CRM database pull in this browser-extension version (a browser can't open a direct SQL Server connection) — use the CSV exports, or the Python/Flask version of this tool for that path.
